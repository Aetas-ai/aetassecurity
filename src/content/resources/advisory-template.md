This draft shows writers the structure of an advisory. It is visible only in placeholder mode and is never built for the live site.

## Summary

Two or three sentences: what the issue is, who it affects, and how urgent it is. Name the affected product and versions exactly.

## Who is affected

The products, versions, and configurations affected, and how a reader can check whether they are exposed.

## What we recommend

Numbered, concrete steps in priority order:

1. The fix or update to apply, with a link to the vendor's guidance.
2. A temporary mitigation, if a fix is not yet available.
3. What to look for in your logs if you suspect the issue was used against you.

## How Aetas Security can help

One short paragraph linking to the relevant service, such as [MXDR](/services/mxdr) or [Incident Response](/services/incident-response). No metrics, customer names, or response-time promises unless they are approved.

## References

Links to the vendor advisory and the CVE record. Use only public, verifiable sources.
